# Placing the dummy head

## Grab and move it

Grab the dummy head with the controller grip to move it. Either hand works. When you let go, it stays where it is.

- **Grab up close**: Bring your hand to the head and squeeze the grip when a light-blue outline appears around it. You can rotate it freely, just like an object in your hand.
- **Point and grab**: Point the beam at the head and squeeze the grip. It keeps facing you as it moves; push the stick up to move it farther away, down to bring it closer, and left or right to shift it sideways.

Check the [in-app binding names](controllers) for your controller.

## Pin in place

Press **Pin in place** in the button row that appears when you point the beam at the head, and the dummy head will not move even if you try to grab it. This prevents moving it by accident after you have positioned it. Press **Pin in place** again to release it.

## Face direction

Change this with **Flip facing** in the button row, or under **Main settings**, in “Face direction when placed.”

- **Front**: The dummy head faces you (default).
- **Back**: The dummy head faces the same direction as you.

It keeps this direction while you point and grab it.

## Opacity

Press **Opacity** in the button row to cycle through four levels: 100→60→30→10%. Under **Main settings**, “Dummy head opacity” allows fine adjustment, including full transparency. If it is fully transparent, it can be hard to locate, so we recommend leaving it slightly visible while positioning it.
