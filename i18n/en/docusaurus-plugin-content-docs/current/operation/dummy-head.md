# Placing the dummy head

## Move it manually

With typical bindings, the dummy head follows your right hand while you hold left X + right A, or both A buttons. Move it to the desired position, then release the buttons. Check the [in-app binding names](controllers) for your controller.

<DummyHeadFigure part="place" />

## Face direction

Change this under **Main settings**, in “Face direction when attached to right hand.”

- **Front**: The dummy head faces you.
- **Back**: The dummy head faces the same direction as you.

<DummyHeadFigure part="facing" />

## Opacity

Adjust the display intensity with “Dummy head opacity.” If it is fully transparent, it can be hard to locate, so we recommend leaving it slightly visible while positioning it.
