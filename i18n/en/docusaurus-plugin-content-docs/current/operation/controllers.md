# Check your controller bindings

VR button names depend on your SteamVR controller bindings. This app's **How to use** page displays the binding names it can read for step 3 (placing the dummy head) and step 5 (toggling spatial audio). Check that display first.

Typical Quest-style controller bindings are:

| Action | Typical buttons |
| --- | --- |
| Follow the right hand to place the dummy head | Hold left X + right A, or both A buttons |
| Toggle spatial audio | Press left Y + right B, or both B buttons |

Other controllers may use different button names. If the app's display does not match your controller, check this app's controller binding in SteamVR. When the app cannot read a binding name, it may keep the default example text.

Turn the wrist menu on or off and select its hand under **Main settings**. Use the wrist icon to open it. The menu may be hidden while the SteamVR dashboard is open. See [common issues](../troubleshooting/common-issues) if it does not appear.
