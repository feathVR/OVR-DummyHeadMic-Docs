# Spatial audio settings

## Toggle spatial audio

Press left-hand Y + right-hand B, or the B buttons on both hands, at the same time to toggle spatial audio processing. The dummy head is white when ON and red when OFF.

## Dummy head type

- **Standard**: For general use, such as conversations with multiple people
- **KU100**: A high-quality character suited to solo streaming and ASMR
- **KEMAR**: A character based on measurements that include the torso

Perception varies from person to person. Compare them with headphones and choose the one that suits your use.

## Room preset

Choose from “No reverb,” small room, bathroom, hall, tunnel, and other presets. We recommend “No reverb” for initial checks and positioning. Strong reverb can make it difficult to judge the direction of the direct sound.

## Output volume

Adjust the volume to avoid clipping, and check the final stream level on the OBS meter as well.
