# Supported configurations and recalibration

## Supported camera placement

Only **Local Anchor** is supported. The following features are not supported:

- World Anchor
- Pin 1–3
- Movement using Holoport or teleport

## After calibration

- Do not switch the camera between front and rear (selfie and outward-facing). If you do, switch it back or calibrate again.
- The tracking range is approximately 2.5 m around the avatar. Sync pauses temporarily outside this range and resumes when you return.
- The app reconnects automatically after changing worlds.
- Calibrate again after changing or reloading your avatar.
- When you end sync, you can place the dummy head manually again with the A button.

## Smooth Movement

During calibration, the app temporarily turns VRChat camera Smooth Movement OFF and checks it. After calibration, it restores the original ON/OFF setting and sets the strength to 5 if it was above 5. Only turn it off manually in VRChat if the app reports that its automatic check failed.
